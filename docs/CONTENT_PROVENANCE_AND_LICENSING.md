# Content provenance and licensing policy

This policy separates software rights from question/source-content rights. The repository currently has no software license; do not infer reuse permission.

## Publication requirements

Every production question must retain:

- stable question/version identifiers and content hash;
- subject, syllabus location, language, difficulty and generation/authoring method;
- authoritative source citation and access/freshness metadata where factual evidence is required;
- deterministic proof metadata where mechanically provable;
- generator provider/model and verifier provider/model identities when AI participates;
- verification basis, confidence, correction history and moderation status;
- source/license/permission status sufficient for the intended use.

Same-model second-pass review is not independent evidence. Content without the required source, proof or named human approval must fail closed.

## Source controls

- Use allow-listed primary/official sources with canonical-host, redirect, content-type, size, timeout, freshness and expiry controls.
- Store short attributed claims and metadata; do not copy protected source works unnecessarily.
- Review terms, database rights and jurisdiction-specific restrictions before adding a source bundle.
- Do not treat public availability as permission to redistribute.
- Keep corrections append-only and propagate invalidated question effects through reviewed data operations.

## Contributions

Contributors must identify the origin and rights for submitted questions, explanations, images and datasets. Do not submit leaked exam material, paywalled copies, personal data, answer keys from unauthorized sources or AI output represented as verified fact. Maintainers may request proof of permission and remove content when rights are unclear.

## Owner decisions required

The repository owner must select an OSI-approved software license and separately define the license/terms for original questions, source-derived facts, explanations and generated artifacts. Until that decision is published, all rights remain reserved by their respective owners.
